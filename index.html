Who We Are

Anvicon Infra Pvt Ltd is a premier infrastructure and construction company dedicated to delivering high-quality, future-ready built environments.

Formalized in November 2025, we carry forward the extensive legacy of our predecessor, Anvikshiki Constructions. We specialize in end-to-end development, combining rigorous engineering with a commitment to sustainability.
